About Our Studio

Capturing Every Moment

Framing Every Memory

Welcome to Ajantha Studio & Frame Works. We specialize in custom photo framing to make your favourite pictures look beautiful. Choose from different frame designs, colours, and sizes to match your home or office. We provide quality framing to preserve your precious memories for years to come.

We also provide pre-wedding photoshoots, marriage function photography, housewarming ceremonies, naming ceremonies, birthday parties, and all kinds of special events and functions. Our goal is to capture your special moments and turn them into beautiful memories that you can cherish forever.
